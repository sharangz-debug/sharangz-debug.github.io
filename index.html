50% Health is used as your personal caution threshold. Health measures margin utilisation, not P/L. Because margin falls as a long stock position falls in value, Health can sometimes rise during a loss if required margin shrinks faster proportionally than account value. Live broker Health can still differ because prices, FX and margin update continuously.
